    From the sun-baked red dirt of the Australian Outback, we embark on a long voyage east across the vast blue expanse of the South Pacific, crossing the International Date Line to make landfall on one of the most isolated island chains on Earth, Hawaii.
    
    Rising dramatically out of the ocean depths, this volcanic archipelago is a landscape forged by fire and ocean currents. Lush, emerald valleys carve through towering volcanic ridges, black and gold sand beaches meet the Pacific surf, and the air is heavy with the scent of plumeria and saltwater. Long before it became a bustling modern tourist hub, Hawaii was a collection of thriving Polynesian island communities, where local chieftains (Aliʻi) guided their villages with a deep respect for both the land and the gods of the sea and volcano.

    It is this historic heritage that we celebrate in our next game - also called Hawaii - a worker placement game in which players send their chieftain around the island gathering resources and developing their village.

    Everything in Hawaii revolves around a tight balancing act between two primary resources: Shells (the local currency used to buy tiles) and Feet (movement points spent as your chief travels further along the island paths). Hawaii is a variable yet satisfying point-salad that requires you to optimise your routes across the island, manage your steps wisely, and guide your village to the best renown!
