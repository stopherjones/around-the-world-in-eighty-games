    From the hyper-modern glass towers and neon lights of Singapore's Marina Bay, we hop south across the Singapore Strait and leap directly over the equator into the sprawling, volcanic archipelago of Indonesia. 

    We arrive in the dense, shadowed undergrowth of Sumatra and Borneo - the third-largest tropical rainforest on Earth - where we are almost assaulted by our surroundings, from the thick, humid atmosphere to the vibrant symphony of the wilderness, rich with the call of exotic birds and mamals. We have arrived in the Hutan, the Indonesian word for forest, and the name of our next game.

    In Hutan, each player is tasked with nurturing their own vibrant patch of the Indonesian jungle. Over nine rounds, you will draft flower and sprout cards from a shared market pool and carefully plot their layout onto a personal rainforest grid. The core mechanics rely on precise spatial planning and matching colors: planting flowers of identical types on top of one another allows them to mature into towering, three-dimensional trees.
    
    The ultimate goal is to craft perfect, harmonious habitats. When you successfully complete a pocket of the forest with matching flora, the trees invite the arrival of Indonesia's most iconic and endangered wildlife—including the playful orangutan, the majestic Sumatran tiger, the rhinoceros hornbill, the cassowary, and the rare Sumatran rhino. It’s a beautifully tactile, satisfyingly strategic balancing act where you must weave connected lines, avoid mixed-color penalties, and optimize ecosystem bonuses to watch a blank canvas bloom into a thriving, complex wildlife sanctuary.
