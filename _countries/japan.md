    From the equatorial heat and dense, tangled rainforests of Indonesia, we retrace our steps somewhat, back north-east past China and the Philippines, and across to Japan, the Land of the Rising Sun.
    
    Japan is a mesmerizing contradiction where ancient whispers meet neon-lit futures. It is a place where thousand-year-old wooden temples stand peacefully in the shadow of towering, glass skyscrapers. Where you can ride the legendary Shinkansen (bullet trains), past the snow-capped silhouette of Mount Fuji, only to disembark minutes later into a quiet village where time has stood completely still.

    Make yourself comfortable as we have four games to make the most of our stay in Japan, and truly explore all this wonderful country has to offer: Let's Go To Japan; The White Castle, King of Tokyo and Kokeshi.

    Let's Go To Japan is a delightful card-drafting game, in which players take on the role of travellers constructing a week-long trip split between Tokyo and Kyoto. Over 13 rounds, you draft activity cards featuring iconic sights, local delicacies, and cultural gems, carefully placing them onto your six-day calendar to balance resources and match daily preferences. The magic happens at the end, when you actually 'go' on your planned holiday, stepping through your itinerary day-by-day from left to right  triggering combos, collecting souvenir points, and scoring based on how smoothly your dream vacation unfolds!

    Next up, some history - Japan’s legendary 'White Castle' (Himeji-jō), is a breathtaking 400-year-old masterpiece widely considered Japan’s most beautifully preserved feudal fortress. Our next game, White Castle, is a tight, strategic Euro game where players control rival clans competing for influence and the Daimyo’s favour. Across just three rounds and nine turns, you draft and place dice around the castle grounds to dispatch courtiers into the noble chambers, position warriors on the ramparts, and send gardeners to tend the tranquil koi ponds. A game in which a single clever placement can trigger a cascading series of bonus actions across your clan board!

    King of TOkyo needs little introduction, players step into the giant, scaly feet of colossal kaiju, giant robots, and terrifying aliens, all brawling for total control of the city. It’s a fast, dice-chucking press-your-luck brawl where you roll Yahtzee-style dice to claw your rivals, heal your wounds, purchase power-ups, or rack up Victory Points.

    Our final game, Kokeshi, dials down the intensity for a serene, tactile abstract puzzle inspired by traditional Japanese craftsmanship. Named after the iconic, hand-carved wooden dolls of northern Japan, Kokeshi is a clever engine-builder centred on track construction. On your turn, you slide a wooden doll along one of your personal workshop tracks, activating every action tile in its path. As you extend and upgrade your tracks with new tiles, your slides trigger satisfying chain reactions—moving other dolls, unlocking quality craft tiles, and generating Happiness Points.
