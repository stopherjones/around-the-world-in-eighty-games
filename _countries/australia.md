    From the vibrant street life and rich heritage of Japan, we embark on a long journey south, crossing the Equator once more and spanning the vast blue expanse of the Pacific to make landfall in the sun-baked, ancient continent of Australia.
    
    Welcome to the Land Down Under - a continent of dazzling contrasts, where endless golden beaches and world-famous ocean reefs give way to the rugged red expanse of the Outback. We trade feudal castles and neon avenues for dusty trails, stunning coastal highways, and the mysterious, star-lit quiet of the bush. As we journey past red sandstone monoliths, eucalyptus forests, and secluded billabongs, the air is filled with the calls of kookabourras and the rustle of unique wildlife. To capture both the thrilling sights of an Australian road trip and the rich, mystical folklore of the Outback, our games for this stop take us on a tour of the continent and into the dreamscapes of its most famous native animals.

    First, we’re grabbing our maps, packing a campervan, and setting off on a whistle-stop tour across the continent in Boomerang: Australia. This light 'draft-and-write' game sees players competing to plan the ultimate Australian holiday over four rounds. Each turn, you draft cards from a shared hand, selecting iconic sights to visit, native animals to spot, and outdoor activities to check off on your personal map score sheet. The strategy lies in card selection and set collection: do you hold onto cards to score huge bonuses for catching throw-and-catch boomerang pairs, or do you draft strategically to complete entire regions of Australia before your rivals?
    
    Next, we venture deep into the Outback as night falls for Dingo's Dreams, a delightfully clever spatial puzzle designed by Alf Seegert. Inspired by native wildlife on Walkabout who have lost their way, players step into the dreamscapes of iconic Australian animals - like Dingo, Koala, Kangaroo, and Emu - to help guide them back home.  Each player manages their own grid of dreamscape tiles. As cards are drawn from a shared deck, you flip matching tiles over to reveal your animal, while continuously sliding new tiles into the grid to shift rows and columns—much like a classic sliding puzzle. Your goal is to be the first to manipulate your board so that your animal tiles form a specific goal pattern. It is a smooth, tactile race of spatial reasoning that feels equal parts soothing and brain-bending as you try to line up your dream path before anyone else!
